## 中文

在浏览器中交互查看和旋转三维体积渲染。首页和模型查看器均提供中英语言切换，并记住你的选择。

1. 打开在线查看器，选择数据集。
2. 等待数据加载；文件较大，首次打开可能需要一些时间。
3. 拖动鼠标旋转，滚轮缩放，按空格暂停或继续播放。其他显示选项可在页面控件中调整。

浏览器需支持 WebGL 2 和 WebM 视频解码。2890 · Cluster 3 还需从 jsDelivr 加载渲染组件，观看时需要能访问该服务。

这套高级交互渲染工具的源码将在之后公开。欢迎为 [volvis 仓库点 Star](https://github.com/S-Yuan137/volvis)，或[关注我的 GitHub](https://github.com/S-Yuan137)，获取后续更新。

## English

Explore and rotate interactive 3D volume renderings in your browser. The gallery and model viewers offer Chinese and English, and remember your language preference.

1. Open the gallery and choose a dataset.
2. Wait for the data to load. These are large files, so the first visit may take a little time.
3. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and press Space to pause or resume. Use the page controls to adjust the display.

Your browser needs WebGL 2 and WebM video decoding. The 2890 · Cluster 3 viewer also loads rendering components from jsDelivr, which must be accessible on your network.

The source code for this advanced interactive rendering tool will be released in the future. [Star the volvis repository](https://github.com/S-Yuan137/volvis) or [follow me on GitHub](https://github.com/S-Yuan137) for updates.
